Run autonomous multi-step web research using Gemini Deep Research and return a synthesized report with citations.

Use this tool when:
- A question requires exhaustive investigation across many sources
- Single websearch queries are insufficient
- You need a structured research report with corroborated claims

Prefer `websearch` for quick factual lookups. Use `deep_research` for open-ended or high-stakes research (market analysis, technology comparisons, due diligence, literature surveys).

The tool runs asynchronously on Google's servers (typically 2–20 minutes). Do not use for simple questions answerable in one search.

Provide a clear research brief. Optionally specify output structure (sections, tables, audience).

Results include citations — verify critical claims before presenting as facts.
